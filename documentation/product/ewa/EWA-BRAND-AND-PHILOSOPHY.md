# EWA brand and product philosophy

EWA means **Empery World Awakening** in this product's brand language. This document defines editorial/product intent. Its philosophical language is a metaphor, not a scientific claim or an implementation acceptance test.

## Brand meaning

Within this brand, “Empery” represents full presence and openness rather than coercive control. “World” represents relationships and change rather than a static map. The intended companion experience is nearby support, clarity and assistance while preserving the user's agency.

EWA is also the name of the server platform/API domain under `/api/v1/ewa`. [Persona vision](EWA-COMPANION-PERSONA-VISION.md) describes the user experience; [taxonomy](EWA-VS-AMKA-TAXONOMY.md) separates it from marketing AMKA.

## Parent and persona family

The parent EWA identity supplies the brand frame; it need not be a separate avatar on every screen. Mela, Zera, Cora, Tala and Feni are named persona/mode concepts for ideas/text, care, connection/planning, step support and focus. Their story roles do not assert that every persona is implemented, released or identical to its code preset. Use [the seed matrix](EWA-COMPANION-SEED-PERSONA-MATRIX.md) and source when deciding technical availability.

These companion concepts do not imply entry into `/api/v1/marketing-cabinet/amka/*`. The persona-family roadmap under `documentation/plans/product/ewa/` is operator-local process material, separate from current contracts.

## Editorial boundaries

Narrative mythology, fairy-tale imagery and expanded brand stories can live in marketing/creative artifacts. Engineering/product canon must state actual promises, allowed fields and policy boundaries. Metaphors must not replace authorization or action constraints, or present consciousness/AGI/subjective experience as established technical facts.

Product decisions still needing their own design evidence include consistent UI tone/naming, warning-versus-blocking behavior and transparent sources for persistent character/preferences (dialogue, preset, organizational policy). The existence of character storage does not settle all of those UX choices. See [proactive automation policy](EWA-COMPANION-PROACTIVE-AUTOMATION.md).
