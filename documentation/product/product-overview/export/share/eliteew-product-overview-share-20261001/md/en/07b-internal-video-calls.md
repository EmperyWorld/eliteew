# P7b — Internal video calls

## Thesis

Internal video lives in the **Video Calls** app; 1:1 often starts from GAF.
Media goes through the product’s internal Meet contour; the call opens **inside**
the product window (not “just a link in a foreign browser” as the only normal path).

**1:1:** the caller does **not** enter the room until the callee **accepts**
(accept first, then join). Explicit statuses (waiting / active / missed) and delivery
of the inbound call to chosen surfaces.

**Where to accept the call:** both surfaces (default) · desk only · mobile only —
a user setting.

**Group calls:** a separate scenario; the organizer’s room-entry rules differ from
the 1:1 accept rules — do not collapse them into one claim.

**Not promised:** “native VR-OS telephony”; VR calls go through the browser layer
(P1a, checked).
