# P5 — Apps, catalog, two AI contours

## App catalog

The desk has a **catalog**: browse, install/remove under organization access,
and an app card. The desk is assembled for the role — not one fixed icon set
for everyone.

**Fleet / App Store** here means “catalog + installed set” in the desktop shell.
Do not confuse it with the host OS store or the host package store.

## Two AI contours — keep them apart

| Contour | Name in UI | Substance | Do not confuse with |
|---------|------------|-----------|---------------------|
| **EWA** | Assistant / companion | Presence and actions on the desk; orchestration | Experts |
| **Experts** | Experts / AI chat | Instrumental chat with files and a “team” | The EWA companion |

**Present:** these are different products on the desk. **Not promised:** “one AI does
everything the same way.”
