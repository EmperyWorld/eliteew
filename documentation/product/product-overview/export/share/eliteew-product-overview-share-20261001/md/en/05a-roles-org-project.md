# P5a — Organization and project roles

**Exemplar:** marketing cabinet + project files

## Model (overview language)

| Role | Meaning | Typical powers |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Owner of the company contour** | Sets the organization frame | Company scope; high access across projects |
| **Project lead** | Runs one project (`created_by` / lead) | Team, modules, sensitive deletes, settings |
| **Participant** | Works in granted modules | Only explicit section rights; empty rights ≠ full write |

Separately: permission to upload into the **project vault** can be granted on
its own and does **not** unlock every cabinet module by itself.

Project invites are controlled by the lead; server-side access checks are
mandatory (not “everyone sees everything”).
