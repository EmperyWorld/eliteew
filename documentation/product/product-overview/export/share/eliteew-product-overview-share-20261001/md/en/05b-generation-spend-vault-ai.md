# P5b — Generation spend control · AI on project data

Heavy generations (images, presentations, music, and others through the shared
generation contour) are accounted as **energy / credits** by generation type.
The UI shows cost before start; if balance is short — a clear refusal, never a
silent success.

This is **in-product spend control**, not a bank billing statement in this pack.

## AI on project data (vault RAG)

In the marketing contour, research and answers can lean on **project vault
documents** (RAG), not on an “internet brain” by default for `imc_rag_*` types.
Quality depends on what is loaded into the vault; thin coverage is surfaced
honestly with prompts.

**Not promised:** “the AI always knows everything about the company with no documents.”
