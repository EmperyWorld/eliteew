# P4 — Desk as the work environment

## Thesis

The EliteEW desk carries windows, icons, a taskbar, a launcher, and apps scoped
to the person’s role. It is an **integrated environment** in the browser (and in
client / FoFline shells — see P10), not a pile of unrelated browser tabs.

Which icons appear depends on **role**, the **app catalog**, and organization
policy — desks are not identical for everyone.

Host / DIY / EliteEW comparisons live briefly in [P1](01-what-is-eliteew.md);
promise boundaries live in [P12](12-honest-limits.md).
