# P7a — Internal messenger (GAF)

## Thesis

**GAF Chat** is the 1:1 messenger inside the contour: threads, files, composer.
Against Shadow IT, the work channel does **not** have to leave into a personal
OS messenger.

By default a chat may be **ordinary** (not E2EE). End-to-end encryption is a
separate mode (P7c), not “every message is always E2EE.”
