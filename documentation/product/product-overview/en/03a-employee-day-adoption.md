# P3a — Employee day: internal adoption

**Diagram:** [D04 — employee day](../assets/diagrams/D04-employee-day.md)  
**Regulation:** pointer only to [P3](03-regulatory-map.md) — no restatement

## Position

EliteEW is not “another SaaS on the side.” It is a **single workday contour** for
an organization employee: desk, files, documents, messaging, AI on project
materials, mobile satellite — with roles and a perimeter.

## One day (swimlane)

1. **Sign-in** — single entry into the work layer (SSO detail — P5c).
2. **Desk** — windows, role-scoped app catalog (P4, P5, P6).
3. **Files and documents** — library, ACL/vault, editor (P4a–b).
4. **Comms** — internal messenger and video calls (P7a–b); phone/VR when needed (P1a, P7).
5. **Marketing / project** — cabinet on project data (if the role allows) (P5 / Must cards).
6. **AI** — inside the contour, not “into a personal external chat” (P5b).
7. **Mobile** — feed/chat/push as a satellite, not the full desk (P7e).
