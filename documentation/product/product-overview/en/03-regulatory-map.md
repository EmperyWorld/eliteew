# P3 — Regulatory orientation map

**Diagram:** [D03 — norms: contributes / stays](../assets/diagrams/D03-npa-split.md)

## Important

This chapter is an **orientation map** for security and counsel dialogue inside
the organization. EliteEW **helps** close an organizational-technical contour
(data separation, access, roles, perimeter). It does **not** replace
certification, protective-tool licensing, impact assessment, or internal policy.

**Forbidden in this pack:** claims of “already certified,” “full legal compliance
out of the box,” guarantees about fines or lawsuits.

Current wording of norms is checked by the organization’s own counsel.

## Orientations (meaning map)

| Orientation | Topic | What EliteEW contributes | What stays with the organization |
|-------------|-------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Information protection | Contour and access | Managed perimeter, roles, work-layer access control | Threat model, certification, tool choice, org measures |
| Cross-border data | Where personal data goes | Storage and work in the **chosen** edition contour (cloud / on-prem) | Legal bases, contracts, registers |
| AI use | Transparency and control | Project-data AI inside the contour; access policies (P5b) | AI policy, risk assessment, operator responsibility |
| Personal data | Protection and separation | Technical separation and access levers | Data controller duties, consents, localization by model |

Exact articles and dates belong in the organization’s legal pack; here only
**meaning**.
