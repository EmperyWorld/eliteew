# P10 — Editions: cloud / on-prem / client

**Diagram:** [D08](../assets/diagrams/D08-editions.md)  
**FoFline:** ≠ air-gap (below)

## Three forms of one product

| Edition | Who holds infrastructure | Meaning |
|---------|--------------------------|---------|
| **Cloud** | Hosted contour | Work in a shared hosted layer (place access is separate from MIT) |
| **On-prem** | Organization | Self-host the gift under MIT, or a contour with optional accompaniment |
| **Client** | Mostly the device | Offline-capable subset; remote tools by allowlist and entitlement |

**Not promised:** “on-prem is one button for anyone without a profile.”

## FoFline (client-adjacent)

A free **local** pack + optional protected channel to the same API.
A local self-host profile starts **without** a service capsule; hosted /
trusted channel may require entitlement. **Not** pure air-gap.
**Not** a second independent backend.

Foundation (entitlement, SSO, shell OTA, local Start) is shipped.
Residual live-smoke / pack parity is **not** masked as “Done” for the whole
contour.
